Six requirements for every solution

What we expect from the tools we list, build and install on farms.

Open source
Code, designs and data are published: anyone can study, change and share them.
Sustainable
An open tool outlives the company that built it, and gets repaired instead of replaced.
Complete
Software, hardware, electronics, data: everything needed for an end-to-end solution.
Affordable
No licence to pay: you pay for service and support, not for the right to use.
Resilient
Installed on the farm, on your own server or with the host of your choice: you depend on no one.
Interoperable
Open formats and shared standards: tools talk to each other and data flows between them.
