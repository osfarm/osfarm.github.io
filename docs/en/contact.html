Personal data

The data controller is the {{ asso.nom }} association, reachable at {{ asso.courriel }}.

  • Contact form. Your message is emailed to the team, who use it to answer you. It is not stored on the site; only your IP address is kept for up to two days, to limit abuse.
  • Training catalogue. Quote requests and operator applications are passed on to the member concerned and kept by the association for follow-up: reminders and evaluation.
  • Membership. It goes through HelloAsso, which processes the payment and the membership data. The association checks a membership from the email address given.
  • Directory assistant. Your question is passed to the association's automation server, then to Mistral AI, a French company, on its European infrastructure, in order to write the answer. No conversation is kept: the thread lives in your browser and disappears when you close the tab. Only your IP address is kept for up to two days, to limit abuse, and the number of questions per day is counted without their content. The assistant answers only from this site's published content.
  • Audience measurement. Google Analytics only loads with your consent, given in the banner and valid for six months. You can withdraw it at any time: manage cookies.
  • Videos. YouTube videos only load if you click on them.

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to {{ asso.courriel }}. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (complaint form in French).
