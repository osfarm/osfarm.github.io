La Vauzelle, our farm

The association’s headquarters is a working farm in the Saintonge area of western France, farmed organically since 2017. This is where the community’s tools are tested, demonstrated and taught, under the conditions of a real farm in Charente: its soils, its climate, its constraints.

{{ asso.siege }}, France

Area
32 ha of certified organic land, 11 ha of it irrigable
Soils
Clay-limestone “groies” and red chestnut soils
Habitats
2 ha of holm oak wood, 1 ha of wetland, a well and a walled vegetable garden
Buildings
3,000 m² of farm buildings and 650 m² of living space
Networks
Fibre, long-range wifi and a LoRa network for sensors
Guidance
An RTK base station on the Centipède network, “LAVZ”, for centimetre positioning
Energy
On-site solar power

REBUS, a FarmLab in the making

OSFarm is preparing a third place for open digital agriculture at La Vauzelle: somewhere to learn, experiment and share free tools on a working farm, not in a lab cut off from the field. Every tool will be documented and reproducible, so that farmers leave with solutions they can maintain themselves.

  • Training and making. Second-hand servers running Linux, sensors and weather stations, installing the community’s software.
  • Research and development. Field trials of GORT, GAIA and AOG+, fed by the data of a digital twin of the farm.
  • Hosting. Two- to five-day bootcamps, groups from chambers of agriculture, agricultural colleges, machinery cooperatives and farm cooperatives, demonstration days.

Discover the REBUS project (in French)

Planned timeline

  1. 2026 Project set-up and building works
  2. 2027 First services, digital twin and first bootcamps
  3. 2028 Consolidation and publication of the model as open source

Provisional budget of €218,700, with the EU’s EAFRD fund through the Pays de Saintonge Romane and the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region; the funding plan is being put together.
